# Product Specification

## Overview

sleepnaked is a wellness application focused on improving sleep quality through
personalized insights, tracking, and guidance.

## Goals

- Help users understand and improve their sleep habits.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on sleep data.
- Deliver a seamless experience across mobile and web platforms.

## Target Users

- Adults looking to improve sleep quality.
- Health-conscious individuals interested in sleep science.

## Core Features

1. **Sleep Tracking** – Log sleep start/end times and quality ratings.
2. **Insights Dashboard** – Visualise trends and patterns over time.
3. **Recommendations** – Personalised tips based on user data.
4. **Reminders** – Configurable bedtime and wake-up reminders.
5. **Progress Reports** – Weekly and monthly summaries.

## Non-Goals (v1)

- Integration with third-party wearables (planned for v2).
- Medical-grade sleep analysis.

## Success Metrics

- 7-day retention rate ≥ 40 %.
- Average session length ≥ 3 minutes.
- User-reported sleep improvement score ≥ 4/5 after 30 days.
