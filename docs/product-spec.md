# Product Specification – Sleep Naked

## Overview

**Sleep Naked** is a wellness application designed to help users improve their sleep quality through evidence-based tracking, personalized recommendations, and actionable insights. The app encourages healthier sleep habits by removing the friction around sleep hygiene.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of people struggle with poor sleep quality due to inconsistent routines, environmental factors, and a lack of personalized guidance. Existing solutions are either too complex or fail to deliver meaningful, actionable recommendations.

---

## Goals

- Help users track and understand their sleep patterns.
- Provide personalized, science-backed recommendations to improve sleep quality.
- Create a simple, delightful experience that users want to engage with nightly.
- Support both mobile and web platforms.

---

## Target Users

| Persona | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **The Stressed Professional** | Ages 25–45, struggles with racing thoughts at bedtime, wants quick wins. |
| **The Health Enthusiast** | Ages 20–40, tracks fitness metrics, wants sleep integrated with their wellness stack. |
| **The New Parent** | Ages 28–40, dealing with disrupted sleep, needs flexibility and understanding. |

---

## Core Features

### MVP (v1.0)

1. **Sleep Logging** – Manual entry of sleep and wake times.
2. **Sleep Score** – A daily score (0–100) derived from duration, consistency, and self-reported quality.
3. **Sleep Trends** – Weekly and monthly charts showing sleep patterns.
4. **Recommendations** – Simple, personalized tips based on logged data.
5. **Reminders** – Configurable bedtime and wake-up reminders.

### Future Releases

- Wearable device integration (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit).
- Smart alarm (wake during lightest sleep phase).
- Sleep journal / mood tagging.
- Community challenges and streaks.
- Premium subscription tier with advanced analytics.

---

## Non-Goals

- Sleep Naked will **not** diagnose sleep disorders (e.g., sleep apnea).
- Sleep Naked will **not** replace medical advice.
- The MVP will **not** support wearable hardware integration.

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| D7 Retention | ≥ 40% |
| D30 Retention | ≥ 20% |

| App Store Rating | ≥ 4.5 stars |

---

## Constraints

- Must comply with GDPR and CCPA privacy regulations.
- Sleep data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
- The app must be accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA compliance).
