# Sleep Naked

A creation-first social platform where identity is expressed through taste, not performance.

Sleep Naked is designed around a simple idea: people connect more meaningfully through what they make and what they resonate with than through curated profiles or follower counts.

---

## Overview

Sleep Naked is a physical-feeling, visually expressive social platform built around:

- Writing as the core primitive
- A corkboard-style identity system
- Taste-based discovery instead of social metrics
- Permanent, intentional interactions

Users create entries, arrange them spatially, and interact through nods, pins, stamps, and annotations.

---

## Core Concepts

### Identity
- Username (single identity)
- Stamp (personal visual signature)
- Corkboard (primary profile surface)
- One-line bio

### Content
- Entries (Thoughts, Journals, Essays, Poems, Fiction, Quotes, Jokes, Dreams)
- Cards (visual representations)
- Cabinet (structured archive)
- Corkboard (freeform surface)

### Interaction
- Nod (light acknowledgment)
- Pin (save to your board)
- Stamp (permanent signature)
- Annotation (contextual response)

### Discovery
- Portal (window into another user's board)
- Aura (visual identity layer)
- Web (network graph of connections)
- Taste-based recommendations
